# Voice Commanding the PRETTY Spacecraft over Amateur Radio

**LA4O technical report, version 1.0 — 30 July 2026**

On 27 July 2026 the ESA OPS-SAT PRETTY spacecraft received a spoken command over the 23 cm amateur
band from LA4O, Oslogruppen av NRRL, transcribed it in orbit and launched DOOM. This is the
technical report documenting the ground-station side of that experiment, written from the
perspective of the radio amateurs who built and operated it.

📄 **[Read the report](index.html)**

A single self-contained HTML document: 38 figures, 22 tables, 130 photographic plates and 48
embedded audio recordings, among them the first audio ever returned from the spacecraft. Click any
image to enlarge; audio plays in place. Prints to A4.
